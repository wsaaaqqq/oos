Feel your pain — session picker only showing a handful of recent ones despite hundreds in the db.

Built a workaround: oos

It reads the OpenCode SQLite db directly, so it searches ALL sessions from
ALL projects at once — no directory scoping, no pagination limits, no
subagent dilution.

- Type a keyword → real-time filter across every project
- ↑/↓ to pick, Enter to resume the conversation
- Multi-keyword AND + ! exclusion
- Zero config, single binary

https://github.com/wsaaaqqq/oos

MIT, one-liner install. Happy to take feedback.
