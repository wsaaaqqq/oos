I ran into the exact same problem -- sessions scattered across projects, no way to find the right one without `cd`-ing around.

I ended up building oos, a standalone TUI tool that reads the OpenCode SQLite db directly and lets you search across ALL sessions from ALL projects at once:

- Type a keyword -> real-time filter across all projects
- ↑/↓ to pick, Enter to resume the conversation
- Multi-keyword AND + ! exclusion
- Zero config, single binary

https://github.com/wsaaaqqq/oos

It's been a lifesaver for my 400+ sessions. Not a full fix for the TUI picker, but a working solution today. Would love feedback if you try it.
